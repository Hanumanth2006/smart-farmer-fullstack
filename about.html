🌾 About Smart Farmer

Connecting farmers directly with buyers through technology.

Our Mission

Smart Farmer is a frontend platform designed to help farmers showcase their crops and connect with buyers digitally.

Our goal is to make agricultural information and crop selling easier, faster and more accessible to farmers.

👨‍🌾

For Farmers

Farmers can display their crops, view market prices and manage their products.

🛒

For Buyers

Buyers can search crops, compare prices and purchase agricultural products.
